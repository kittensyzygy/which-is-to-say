Which is to say

“I miss you.”

“Remember the time we danced at the Exhange and rode home in the rain?”

“Tonight’s strong memory is sitting in Giaco’s place, my back and bum on a hard pew, legs stretched out; you
standing, leaning on your elbows, leafing through the book of Partisan women, telling me stories of resistance. (It feels like treasure.)”

“You bind me in fabric, spin me like a top, our breath touching.” I missed you.

I put my hand in my pocket and rub the ocean coin you gave me for safe passage.

“Want any dinner?”

Which is to say,

I love you.
